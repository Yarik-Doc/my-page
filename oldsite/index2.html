About Me

    Hello! My name is Yaroslav Hryhoryev, and I specialize in the repair and maintenance of operating room equipment, sterilization equipment, electronic boards, and X-ray equipment. With ten years of experience in this field, I have acquired professional skills and gained valuable expertise.

    In addition to my technical expertise, I am also an avid learner in the field of computer science. I actively study programming languages such as Python, Bash, and PHP, exploring their applications in various projects. This allows me to stay up to date with the latest technologies and incorporate them into my work. Furthermore, I have a keen interest in UI & UX design. I am currently studying and honing my skills in creating user-friendly and visually appealing interfaces. With a focus on creating seamless user experiences, I aim to combine my technical knowledge with design principles to deliver innovative solutions.

    My portfolio showcases successful projects in the repair and technical servicing of operating room equipment, sterilization equipment, electronic boards, and X-ray equipment. The "OPERATING ROOM" section displays photographs of projects that I have successfully executed, highlighting the quality of my work and my professional approach.

    I am dedicated to providing high-quality service and ensuring customer satisfaction. With my troubleshooting abilities and expertise in various programming languages, I can effectively address technical challenges and offer efficient solutions.

    Thank you for your interest in my work. Please feel free to reach out to me to discuss your needs and requirements. I am excited to collaborate with you and leverage my skills in equipment repair, programming, and UI & UX design to meet your expectations.

Best regards, Yaroslav Hryhoryev
